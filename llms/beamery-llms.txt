# Beamery

> Beamery is an AI-powered talent lifecycle management and workforce transformation platform. It unifies fragmented talent data to enable skills-based hiring, candidate relationship management (talent CRM), workforce planning, internal talent mobility, and talent marketing for enterprises. HR-tech / recruitment technology.
